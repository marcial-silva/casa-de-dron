Good to Know

De Machinist is about 15 minutes from Rotterdam Centraal.

🚕 Taxi or rideshare: approx. 10 min from the station.
🚲 Rotterdam is very bike-friendly — rentals available city-wide.

Think a warm summer evening on holiday — elegant, light, and effortless. We'd love to see soft pastel tones: blush, sage, powder blue, warm ivory, or lilac.
